Re: Submission of manuscript, “Allocative Efficiency and Regulatory Enforcement Optimization in Professional Councils via Multilevel Longitudinal Analysis”

Dear Editors,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled “Allocative Efficiency and Regulatory Enforcement Optimization in Professional Councils via Multilevel Longitudinal Analysis” for consideration in *Public Management Review*.

This study examines how a frontline regulatory organization can translate routine administrative records into actionable metrics for operational and strategic public management decisions under capacity constraints. Using longitudinal administrative data (2016–2023) aggregated to territorial units, we assess allocative efficiency in regulatory inspections by combining indicators of market growth, inspection intensity, and outcomes (non-compliance detection).

We show that market expansion and enforcement pressure are territorially uneven; that operational shocks (COVID-19) are followed by measurable re-composition of activity; and that inspection “case-mix” functions as a pragmatic proxy of enforcement stage, supporting risk-based planning and performance management.

Our manuscript contributes to PMR’s focus on strategic and operational management of public services and on policy implementation by offering an empirically grounded, reproducible monitoring framework with direct implications for performance management, resource allocation, and territorial targeting. The contribution is not framed as a causal claim, but as a practical measurement and decision-support approach for inspection systems.

We confirm that this work is original, not under review elsewhere, and that all authors meet Taylor & Francis authorship criteria. The authors report no competing interests. Funding, data availability, and ethics-related statements are provided in the submission files.

Thank you for considering our submission. We look forward to the opportunity to contribute to PMR’s ongoing dialogue on public management, regulatory governance, and enforcement capacity.
