Dear Editor,

On behalf of my co-authors, I am pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "Chemical Architecture, Mechanical Reliability, and Environmental Implications of Natural Fiber Geotextiles for Erosion Control" for consideration in the International Journal of Geosynthetics and Ground Engineering. This comprehensive review analyzes the structural properties and environmental performance of natural fiber geotextiles, with a focus on their application in erosion control systems. By integrating findings from materials science, geotechnical engineering, and environmental studies, this work provides novel insights into the sustainable potential of plant-based geosynthetics as alternatives to conventional synthetic materials.

Our manuscript presents a systematic evaluation of natural fiber degradation mechanisms, mechanical behavior under soil-stress conditions, and life-cycle environmental impacts. Field-based case studies demonstrate how these materials perform in real-world erosion control scenarios across different climatic conditions. We believe this work will be of significant interest to your readership, particularly in the areas of geosynthetic materials, sustainable civil engineering, soil conservation, and natural fiber composites.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published before, and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the final version and agreed with its submission. Thank you for considering our work for publication, and we look forward to your response.
