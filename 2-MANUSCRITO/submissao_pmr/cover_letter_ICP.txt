Subject: Submission of a review article for consideration

Dear Editor,

We hereby submit the review manuscript entitled “Chemical Architecture, Mechanical Reliability, and Environmental Implications of Natural Fiber Geotextiles for Erosion Control” for consideration in Industrial Crops and Products.

This review consolidates experimental and theoretical evidence linking lignocellulosic chemical architecture (with emphasis on lignin/cellulose ratio and supramolecular organization) to biodegradation kinetics and the Functional Service Life of natural fiber geotextiles under tropical exposure. In addition, the manuscript frames durability as a design variable through probabilistic reliability (Weibull P10), directly aligned with the temporary-performance requirement of erosion control during the critical window for vegetative establishment, while addressing the environmental liabilities associated with fossil-based geosynthetics.

The topic is within the scope of Industrial Crops and Products because it positions renewable plant fibers as engineered products for bio-based geotechnical applications, integrating material chemistry, processing (e.g., alkaline mercerization and surface coatings), and functional performance across time.
